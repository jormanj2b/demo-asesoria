Services & pricing

Flat fees, no surprise bills

We don’t bill for every call or question. If you need something outside your plan one month, we quote it first.

Freelancers

$95/mo

  • Quarterly estimated taxes (1040-ES)
  • Monthly bookkeeping in QuickBooks
  • Annual return with Schedule C included
  • Same-day replies by email or text

Up to 60 transactions a month. Above that, $125/mo.

Small businesses

$295/mo

  • Full bookkeeping and monthly reconciliations
  • Business tax return (1120-S or 1065)
  • Colorado sales tax filings
  • Quarterly results meeting

For LLCs and S corps with up to 200 transactions a month.

Payroll

$45/mo + $6 per employee

  • Direct deposit and pay stubs
  • Federal and state payroll tax filings
  • W-2s and 1099s at year-end
  • New-hire reporting

Includes up to 2 pay runs a month.

Starting a business

A one-hour meeting to decide whether you should stay a sole proprietor, form an LLC or elect S corp status, using numbers from your own situation. Then we handle the paperwork: EIN, your Colorado LLC filing, state tax registration and the S corp election if it makes sense.

Frequently asked questions

Can I switch to you in the middle of the year?

Yes. We get the records from your previous accountant and review what’s already been filed this year at no charge.

Do I have to come to the office?

No. Most clients see us once a year. Everything else happens by email, phone or video call.

What if the IRS sends me a letter?

Forward it to us and we’ll respond. Notices about returns we prepared are included in your fee.

Not sure which plan fits?

Send us a note and we’ll tell you within one business day.
